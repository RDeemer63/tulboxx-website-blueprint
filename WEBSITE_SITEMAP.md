**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Canonical  
**Purpose:** Define the architecture, purpose, and flow of the TULBOXX marketing website.

---

# Introduction

The TULBOXX website is not a collection of pages.

It is one continuous story.

Each page exists to answer the next logical question in the customer's mind.

Visitors should never feel overwhelmed.

They should simply continue moving forward because each page creates enough confidence to naturally explore the next.

The website should feel less like browsing software and more like taking a guided tour.

# Later-Phase Content Pages

Resources, Customer Stories, and About belong in the long-term website architecture, but they are not part of the completed core launch blueprint. Develop them only when TULBOXX has approved educational content, verified customer evidence, and the company information needed to make each page useful and credible.

Start Free is a conversion flow rather than a content blueprint. Its route and account-creation steps still require implementation decisions, but the offer is established: no credit card is required; the first five AI-assisted estimate generations are free; drafting does not consume a generation; and the paywall appears when a user selects **Generate Estimate** on the sixth estimate, before AI runs. The current paid offer is the $39-per-month Ground Floor Rate with every feature included and the monthly rate protected for the life of the active subscription. See `PRICING.md` for the complete copy, entitlement rules, and unresolved edge cases.

# SEO Philosophy

Every page should target one primary search intent.

Every page should answer one primary question.

Avoid pages with overlapping purposes.

One page.

One goal.

---

# CTA Philosophy

Every page should have:

One Primary CTA

One Secondary CTA

No competing actions.

Reduce decision fatigue.

---

# Navigation Philosophy

Visitors should always know:

Where they are.

What comes next.

Why it matters.

Navigation should never interrupt the story.

It should support it.

---

# Website Success

The website succeeds when visitors naturally move through the pages without feeling sold.

Every page should create enough trust to make the next page feel obvious.

By the time visitors reach Pricing, they should already believe TULBOXX is the right solution.

Pricing simply confirms the decision.

---

# Final Principle

The sitemap is not navigation.

It is the architecture of belief.

Every page exists to move visitors one step closer to imagining a better business.

If a page does not contribute to that journey, it does not belong.
