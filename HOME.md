# Page Purpose

The homepage introduces TULBOXX without trying to explain everything.

Its job is to help a service business owner immediately recognize their world, imagine a more organized way to run the business, and become curious enough to take the next step.

The visitor should understand three things:

1. TULBOXX is built for contractors and service business owners.
2. It connects the work around customers, estimates, jobs, invoices, and payments.
3. It helps the business feel more organized without creating more administration.

The homepage should create recognition before explanation, outcomes before features, and confidence before commitment.

## Editorial Ownership

This page owns the contractor's current administrative burden and the promise of a better future. It may preview connected work, product philosophy, field use, and capabilities, but it should not fully explain them. Those ideas belong to Platform, Why TULBOXX, How It Works, and Features.

> What is TULBOXX, who is it for, and why should I care?

# Global Page Rules

- The contractor is the hero. TULBOXX is the guide.
- Lead with the visitor's reality before showing the product.
- Use short sentences that read naturally on a phone.
- Never call TULBOXX “just a CRM.”
- Never lead with AI.
- Never present an unverified number, testimonial, rating, or customer count.
- Show product UI only when it explains a real workflow.
- Use one primary action and one secondary action throughout the page.
- The page should feel calm, capable, and direct.
- Every image must have a communication job.

# Visual System for the Homepage

## Canonical Reference

Use [`docs/TULBOXX_BRAND_STYLE_GUIDE.pdf`](docs/TULBOXX_BRAND_STYLE_GUIDE.pdf) as the visual reference for brand styling, colors, typography, and logo treatment.

## Confirmed Core Colors

- Steel Blue: `#082638`
- Tradesman White: `#F2F1F1`
- Charcoal Black: `#2C2C2C`

Accent colors appear in the brand guide but require confirmed digital values before implementation. Do not sample final production values from a compressed screenshot.

## Typography Direction

- Rajdhani Bold: display headlines and selected high-impact labels
- Poppins: body copy, navigation, buttons, and supporting text
- Preserve readability over stylistic consistency if a weight or size performs poorly on small screens.

## Color Rhythm

- Use Steel Blue as the primary confidence color.
- Use Tradesman White to create clarity and breathing room.
- Use accent colors sparingly for actions, progress, and emphasis.
- Do not turn every section into a separate color theme.
- Maintain accessible contrast in all states.

## Photography Direction

The full image set should feel like one honest working world.

Prioritize:

- Realistic field conditions
- Specific trades and tools
- Trucks and equipment with believable wear
- Natural light and weather
- Contractors actively doing or managing work
- Phones and tablets used naturally
- Finished work and customer context

Avoid:

- Generic stock-business gestures
- Perfectly clean clothing and equipment
- Random sparks, smoke, or dramatic weather
- Unnecessary shallow focus that hides the work
- Repeating the same person as multiple customers or trades
- Generated text, logos, or product screens

## Product UI Direction

- Use approved product captures as the source.
- Create fictional demonstration records rather than using real customer data.
- Show one meaningful action per view.
- Keep text large enough to understand.
- Prefer continuity across screens over a gallery of unrelated dashboards.
- Do not redesign the product inside marketing mockups without product approval.

## Motion Direction

- Motion explains relationships, workflow, or progress.
- Motion never delays access to the copy or CTA.
- Scroll behavior must remain understandable without animation.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Avoid constant looping motion near body copy.

# Mobile Experience

- Preserve the full promise and both hero CTAs above the first major visual transition.
- Keep paragraphs short enough to scan between jobs.
- Stack recognition moments as a story, not a dense card grid.
- Show one product screen at a time.
- Keep buttons large and clearly separated.
- Do not require hover to understand an interaction.
- Avoid horizontal carousels unless swipe behavior is obvious and all content remains reachable.
- Test in bright-light conditions and at increased text sizes.

---

# Accessibility Requirements

- Use one semantic `h1` for the homepage promise.
- Maintain a logical heading order.
- Provide meaningful alt text for informative images.
- Use empty alt text for truly decorative textures.
- Ensure all actions are keyboard accessible.
- Provide visible focus states.
- Meet WCAG AA contrast requirements at minimum.
- Never place essential copy only inside an image.
- Caption or transcribe meaningful video and audio.
- Provide static alternatives for workflow animations.
- Respect reduced-motion and data-saving preferences where possible.

---

# Performance Requirements

Performance is part of the brand.

- Prioritize the hero headline and CTA over the hero image.
- Reserve media dimensions to prevent layout shift.
- Serve responsive image sizes and modern formats.
- Load below-the-fold photography and product media only as needed.
- Avoid a heavyweight video hero.
- Do not ship multiple full-resolution product captures when one focused crop explains the idea.
- Measure animation cost on mid-range mobile devices.

# Homepage Acceptance Criteria

The homepage is ready for production when:

- A contractor can explain what TULBOXX is after viewing the hero.
- The opening sections create recognition before introducing product detail.
- Every section has one clear question and one clear job.
- Outcomes appear before features.
- The connected workflow is understandable without animation.
- AI appears as practical assistance with a visible contractor review step.
- No product, pricing, trial, or customer claim is unverified.
- Product visuals use approved screens and fictional demonstration data.
- Photography feels specific to real field work.
- The mobile experience preserves the story instead of merely stacking desktop sections.
- Every CTA uses one of the two approved actions.
- The page naturally encourages the visitor to see how TULBOXX works or start free.
- Every Start Free reassurance accurately states the five-estimate limit and no-credit-card requirement.
- Accessibility, performance, and reduced-motion requirements are met.
