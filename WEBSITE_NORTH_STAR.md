# TULBOXX Website North Star

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Canonical  
**Purpose:** Define the purpose, philosophy, and experience of the TULBOXX marketing website.

---

# Why This Website Exists

The purpose of the TULBOXX website is not to sell software.

Its purpose is to help hardworking service business owners believe there is a better way to run their business.

Most contractors don't struggle because they aren't good at their trade.

They struggle because they're trying to run a growing business with disconnected tools, paperwork, forgotten follow-ups, scattered notes, late invoices, and not enough hours in the day.

TULBOXX exists to remove that friction.

The website should help visitors recognize themselves in that story before introducing the product.

---

# The Promise

We believe contractors should spend more time building their business and less time managing paperwork.

Every page should reinforce one simple promise:

> Run Your Entire Service Business From One Place.

That statement is more than a headline.

It is the lens through which every design decision should be evaluated.

# The Low-Risk First Step

**Start Free** should feel like the natural next step, not a leap of faith.

Visitors can begin without a credit card and generate their first five AI-assisted estimates free. Building, editing, and saving an estimate does not consume a generation. They can build a sixth estimate; the upgrade decision appears only when they select **Generate Estimate**, before AI runs.

If they continue, the current offer is the **Ground Floor Rate**: $39 per month with every feature included. The monthly rate remains protected for the life of the active subscription.

This sequence matters because it lets the contractor judge TULBOXX through real work before making a purchase decision. Do not add manufactured urgency, hidden restrictions, or competing plans to the launch journey.
