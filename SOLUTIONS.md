# Page Purpose

The Solutions page helps visitors recognize that TULBOXX can fit their trade, company stage, and most important business problem.

The Features page answered what TULBOXX can do.

This page answers whether those capabilities make sense for the way a specific contractor works.

The hub should route visitors into genuinely specific solution pages. It must not create the illusion of personalization by swapping trade names over identical copy.

## Editorial Ownership

This page owns relevance: whether TULBOXX fits the visitor's trade, company stage, and desired outcome. Generic platform, workflow, and feature explanations belong on their respective pages; Solutions should show only what changes when those capabilities meet a specific audience.

# Global Page Rules

- Let visitors identify by trade, company stage, or problem.
- Use trade-specific language, workflows, product examples, and imagery.
- Never duplicate a page and replace only the trade name.
- Never claim trade expertise without credible detail.
- Show how one connected platform adapts without pretending every trade works the same way.
- Publish only solution pages with enough original value to deserve their own page.
- Use approved product capabilities and realistic fictional trade data.
- Use real customer proof when available; never fabricate it.
- Lead naturally to Pricing after relevance is established.

# Section 6 - One Platform, Different Paths

## Question Answered

How can the same product fit different work?

## Copy

**Eyebrow**

The work decides the path.

**Headline**

TULBOXX connects the business without forcing every job through the same process.

**Body copy**

An excavation estimate, a painting walkthrough, and an HVAC service call do not begin or move the same way.

They still need a customer, clear work context, a next action, professional communication, and a reliable path to payment.

TULBOXX provides the shared system while the path stays specific to the work.

## Example Paths

```text
Excavation
Call → Site Visit → Scope → Estimate → Approval → Schedule → Work → Invoice → Payment

Painting
Walkthrough → Photos and Rooms → Estimate → Approval → Schedule → Work → Completion → Invoice

HVAC Service
Call → Schedule → Diagnose → Repair or Recommendation → Invoice → Payment → Follow-up
```

## Editorial Boundary

Show only the trade-specific differences that make each path useful. `HOW_IT_WORKS.md` owns the complete generic first-call-to-payment demonstration.

## Visual Direction

**Asset ID:** `SOLUTIONS-PATHS-06`

Use three parallel workflows with shared anchors. Every path must be verified against current product behavior before publication.

# Individual Trade Page Template

Every published trade page should include:

1. Trade-specific hero and outcome promise
2. Recognition of how that trade's work actually begins
3. Two to four common workflow examples
4. Product demonstration using trade-relevant fictional data
5. Outcomes tied to real operational pressure
6. Simple-versus-complex work treatment where relevant
7. Authentic proof or approved product evidence
8. Pricing transition

## Required Research

- Common job types
- Customer journey
- Estimate and scope language
- Scheduling realities
- Equipment, material, and site context
- Documentation needs
- Payment patterns
- Common administrative friction
- Search intent and terminology

## Content Guardrail

Research should improve accuracy, not produce stereotypes. A trade page should be reviewed by someone familiar with that work before publication.

# Accessibility and Performance

- Use one semantic `h1` and logical headings.
- Provide meaningful alt text identifying relevant work context.
- Do not use color alone to distinguish solution types.
- Make filters and routing keyboard accessible.
- Provide static alternatives for workflow animation.
- Meet WCAG AA contrast at minimum.
- Use responsive images and lazy-load below-the-fold trade media.
- Avoid loading full-resolution imagery for every future trade.
- Keep the page useful if interactive routing is unavailable.

# Page Acceptance Criteria

The page is ready when:

- Visitors can identify by trade, company stage, or outcome.
- Featured trades have original workflows, copy, examples, and imagery.
- No page relies on simple trade-name substitution.
- Product demonstrations use verified capabilities.
- Different business sizes are respected without presenting growth as mandatory.
- Proof is real and properly attributed.
- The page remains clear without interaction or motion.
- View Pricing feels like the natural next step.
